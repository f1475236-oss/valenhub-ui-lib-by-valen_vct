# License & Terms

Valen UI is released **free to use**. By loading or including it in your script, you agree to the terms below.

## You may

* Use Valen UI in your own scripts and hubs, free or paid.
* Rebrand the window — change the font, theme colors, accent and title — using the built-in [override options](window/theming.md).
* Share your script with others, with Valen UI loaded from the official loader.

## You may not

* **Resell or redistribute the library itself** — you cannot sell, re-upload, or hand out Valen UI as your own download, paid or free.
* **Claim it as your own.** Do not remove or rename the library to present it as your own UI library.
* **Modify and republish the source.** You may override the theme at runtime, but you may not edit the library's code and release the edited version.
* **Rehost the loader.** Load Valen UI from the official source only; do not copy it onto your own host and distribute that.

## Credit

If you release a public hub built on Valen UI, a small credit (e.g. *"UI by Valen"*) is appreciated but not required.
